# Ideology formation — question script (Phase 1-6)

Ask **in text** at each Phase (see the dialogue-flow policy in SKILL.md). Do not throw everything at once; take the person's answer and dig deeper. Do not lead — pick up the person's raw words.

## Phase 1: Dig into the "why" from first principles

Socratic 6 steps (clarification → challenging assumptions → evidence → alternative viewpoints → consequences → meta) + 5 Whys.

- Why **should** this product/service **exist**? ("Because it makes money" is forbidden — dig beneath it)
- Name 3 premises this industry treats as "**given**". Are they laws of physics, or just shared beliefs?
- If this **did not exist in the world**, whose what would be in trouble?
- What is the status quo you **truly cannot forgive**? (Anger is the seed of ideology)
- (5 Whys) Repeat "why?" 5 times to reach a single sentence you can dig no further beneath

→ Output: **the WHY core (1-2 sentences)**

## Phase 2: Archetype identification

Details in [`archetypes.md`](archetypes.md).

- When you describe the product/world, what are the **words that recur**? (collect and present them)
- Which of these do you respond to most?: knowing the truth (Sage) / creating beauty (Creator) / overcoming hardship (Hero) / breaking the order (Outlaw) / realizing the impossible (Magician) / going to the unexplored (Explorer) / making the standard (Ruler) / protecting (Caregiver)
- Three companies/people you respect. What do they have in common? (a hint for the dominant type)

→ Output: **dominant type + secondary type + the ideological direction that type implies**

## Phase 3: Anti-goals declaration

- **In 10 years, in what state would you regret having "sold your soul"?**
- Even if an investor/boss tells you "**doing this is a sure sell**", what would you **absolutely refuse** because it goes against the ideology?
- What will you **stubbornly refuse to do** even though every competitor does it?
- Like Basecamp, which refused to go upmarket to enterprise in order to protect a "calm way of working", **what do you discard to protect what**?

→ Output: **3-5 anti-goal declarations** (they speak the ideology more strongly than "what you will do")

## Phase 4: Aesthetic Signal

- If your product were sitting in a room, **what would it assert without saying a word**?
- Monochrome or colorful, silent or verbose — with each choice, **what do you want to declare**?
- Put the feel / tone into **one word**. Why that word?

→ Output: **the feel/tone in one word + the reason** (refer to the encoding table in [`archetypes.md`](archetypes.md))

## Phase 5: Retroactive consistency + Tribe

Retroactive consistency (weaving past accumulation into a consistent story — sensemaking, not deception):
- If you retell your past pivots/failures as **one consistent exploration**, what is it?
- What is the **single underlying line** that lets you say "I was aiming for this from the start"?

Tribe (defined by a values cluster):
- Who feels "**this is about me**" — what **values** does that person hold? (by belief, not age/gender)
- If that person, on encountering your product, were to cry "**finally, someone gets me**", what would it be in response to?

→ Output: **one consistent-narrative paragraph + Tribe definition**

## Phase 6: Crystallization

1. Distill the above into the **5-element CONCEPT.md** (WHY / anti-goals / Tribe / Aesthetic Signal / North Star)
2. Finalize the **North Star** (qualitative): "**who, in what state**, equals success" (numbers come after)
3. Check against the **7-item empathy gate** in [`empathy-and-ethics.md`](empathy-and-ethics.md) → if it does not pass, go back to the relevant Phase
4. Present it as an **adopted-interpretation report**, the user finalizes → save + CLAUDE.md @import

## Minimal questions for light mode (for experiments)

- **What belief/hypothesis are you testing?** (1 line)
- **Success = what state?** (1 line)

Write just these 2 lines into CONCEPT.md, and promote to full once productionization is decided.
