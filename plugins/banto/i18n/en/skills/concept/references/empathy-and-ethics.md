# Empathy gate + ethical boundary

Before finalizing CONCEPT.md, always check whether the ideology **generates empathy** and whether it **crosses an ethical line**.

## Empathy gate (7 items)

Check against CONCEPT.md in Phase 6. If even one is clearly NG, go back to the relevant Phase.

1. **Hero test** (StoryBrand): is the hero of the story the **customer**? (Make sure the product/company has not become the protagonist. The brand is the guide.)
2. **Identity resonance** (Oyserman's Identity-Based Motivation): does it touch an identity where the Tribe feels "**this is about me**"
3. **SUCCESs** (Made to Stick): does it satisfy at least 3 of Simple / Unexpected / Concrete / Credible / Emotional / Story
4. **ethos / pathos / logos balance** (Aristotle): is trust (ethos) the foundation, does emotion (pathos) move people, and can logic (logos) justify it
5. **Narrative Transportation**: can the ideology immerse people as a **story** (does it end as a bulleted list of features)
6. **Stickiness test**: can you state the North Star and WHY in **10 words or fewer** (if not, it is not sharp)
7. **Ethics test (TARES)**: does it satisfy the 5 criteria below

## TARES test (5 criteria for ethics)

The ideology/message must satisfy:
- **T**ruthful
- **A**uthentic (rooted in genuine conviction)
- **R**espectful (respects the other party)
- **E**quity (fair / does not exploit information asymmetry)
- **S**ocial responsibility

## Forbidden (dark patterns)

Do not cross the line between the "**manipulation**" of empathy and "**authentic resonance**":
- Fake scarcity / fake social proof (fake urgency / fake reviews)
- Stoking fear and anxiety to rob people of judgment
- Hard-to-cancel flows / confirmshaming ("No, I don't want to succeed"-style)
- Exploiting parasocial relationships (deliberately creating dependence)
- Pretending to hold an ideology or track record you do not have

## Principles of where to draw the line

| Authentic resonance | Manipulation |
|---|---|
| Clearly conveying genuine conviction | Faking / fabricating conviction |
| Offering information the person can choose from themselves | Robbing them of choice / rushing them |
| Calling to values honestly | Artificially creating anxiety / fear |
| "Those it doesn't fit may leave" | A design that lets no one escape |

**The skill's iron rule**: the ideology **originates from the human**. The AI elicits and sharpens it, and guards it with the ethics gate. **The AI does not fabricate empathy.**
